Chapter 1: The Beginning

The rain fell steadily against the windows of the Gryffindor common room, creating a soothing rhythm that filled the otherwise quiet space. Harry sat curled up in his favorite armchair by the fire, a worn leather-bound book resting in his lap.

It had been weeks since the final battle, and Hogwarts was slowly rebuilding—not just the physical structures damaged in the war, but the hearts and spirits of those who remained. The castle felt different now, quieter somehow, as if it too was recovering from old wounds.

"Still reading that old thing?" Hermione's voice broke through his thoughts as she settled into the chair across from him, her own stack of books threatening to topple over.

Harry glanced up from the pages, a small smile tugging at his lips. "It's fascinating, actually. Professor McGonagall found it in the restricted section. It talks about ancient magical techniques that have been lost for centuries."

"Lost for a reason, probably," Ron chimed in, sprawling across the couch with considerably less grace than Hermione had shown. "Nothing good ever comes from the restricted section."

But Harry wasn't so sure about that. The techniques described in the book weren't dark magic—they were something else entirely. Something that resonated with him in a way he couldn't quite explain.

"The path of true mastery lies not in the spell itself, but in understanding the fundamental nature of magic. Only then can one reshape reality according to their will."

The words seemed to echo in his mind, and Harry found himself reading them over and over again, each time discovering new layers of meaning.
